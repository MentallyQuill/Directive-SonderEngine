# Mission Guide

Mission is where Directive stores hard requirements and consequences.
Read this before major decisions.

## Mission structure

Each mission exposes:

- mission title and summary
- primary objectives
- optional objectives
- conditional objectives that appear when revealed
- known facts and available support
- outcome dimensions
- transition summary

## Objective classes

Primary objectives are required to close the mission.
Optional objectives improve outcome quality and resilience.
Conditional objectives unlock after setup facts are revealed.

## Outcome handling

Mission outcomes are recorded as dimensions, not a simple pass/fail flag.
Typical outcomes include:

- partial completion
- informed failure
- transfer conditions
- safety/capital cost implications
- role and chain effects on later missions

## Time and deadlines

Deadlines appear only when known from mission state.
Directive does not invent hidden countdown UI.

## Settlement language

Mission outcomes are shown when the system accepts a valid source.
Some outcomes leave mission in a transfer or continued mode instead of hard fail.
