# People and Command Bearing

People is your public command-record interface.
Only recognized, sourced people appear with full records.

## People records

A person record may include:

- role and service summary
- assignment history
- operational posture
- defining moments

Observed contacts without enough structure appear with limited details.
Directive does not invent private motivation, romance, or hidden future plot.

## Command Bearing

Command Bearing is a campaign reserve.
It represents earned, bounded tactical advantage from meaningful command play.

Current interface behavior:

- the reserve balance is visible in Mission and People views
- automatic checks may consume it when authored conditions are met
- manual spend controls are not fully exposed in every UI state yet

## Best use pattern

Use Command Bearing as a read signal:

- track when you are currently at advantage
- watch outcome summaries for its effect
- treat it as a reward track rather than a hard token that must be spent immediately
