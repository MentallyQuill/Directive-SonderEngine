# First Campaign Workflow

1. Open a story in your host and launch Directive.
2. On Campaign, complete each commissioning step:
   - identity
   - service
   - command profile
   - review
3. Pick `Command` or `Exploration` simulation mode.
4. Start the campaign and wait for the story to open.
5. Read the opening scene and play in normal roleplay prose.
6. Continue your roleplay as normal player text.
7. Keep Mission, People, and Ship open to read required story facts.
8. Use Host features such as reroll or replay only when needed for alternate outcomes.
9. Continue from major checkpoint points or mission pivots as your host provides.

## Practical turn rhythm

Your roleplay is player-authored text.
The system evaluates whether your text can legally move mission, people, and ship state.

Use this rhythm:

- write one clear player turn
- let the host produce the next assistant response
- check Mission and People for any new outcome facts
- play the next scene with those facts

## Early onboarding checks

Before continuing a long sequence, verify:

- the campaign opened
- Mission shows current mission title
- Ship shows crew and cohesion
- People shows known staff records
- Settings shows active authority and model ownership

## Useful habit

Do not manually edit campaign JSON files.
Use host recovery and Directive recovery entry points only if state looks inconsistent.
